ReelFactory

A personal project for creating and editing AI-assisted short videos.

ReelFactory runs as a local dashboard. Its workflows can use third-party AI services to generate scripts and video, and it supports publishing reviewed videos to Instagram. TikTok integration is under development.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026. ReelFactory is a local personal project. The dashboard can store generated scripts, images, videos, configuration and publishing records on the device running it.

When a user chooses a connected service, relevant prompts, images, video or account credentials may be sent to that provider to perform the requested operation. Providers process data under their own privacy policies. Do not include sensitive information in prompts unless you intend to share it with the selected provider.

Access credentials should be stored privately and kept out of public repositories. Users can remove local files and revoke connected-app access through the relevant provider. TikTok integration is under development; this policy will be updated to describe its actual data handling before release.

Questions: contact the project owner through GitHub.
